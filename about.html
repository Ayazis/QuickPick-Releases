About QuickPick

A local Windows action launcher focused on speed, precision, and repeatable execution.

What QuickPick Does

QuickPick executes predefined actions from a cursor-centered UI. You configure actions once, then trigger them instantly through directional muscle memory.

Design Priorities

  • Deterministic behavior over discovery-based workflows
  • Low visual noise and fast target acquisition
  • Local-first configuration and execution
  • Minimal overhead during repeated daily use

System Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • 4GB RAM minimum
  • 100MB free disk space
  • .NET Framework 4.7.2 or later
