A focused set of features designed to keep your hands on the keyboard and your mind on the work.

⚡

Pop-up UI at your cursor

Trigger QuickPick with a custom key or mouse combo and it appears exactly where your cursor is — no hunting for a taskbar.

🪟

App thumbnails & fast switching

See live thumbnails of every open window, peek inside, and jump to any app instantly — like a supercharged Alt+Tab.

🎛️

Built-in system controls

Adjust brightness, control volume, skip tracks, or minimize your current window — all without leaving your keyboard.

🖥️

Remote Desktop compatible

QuickPick keeps working on your local machine even when you're inside a full-screen RDP session. No more getting trapped.
